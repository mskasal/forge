# plain

Super-normal everyday objects: soft volumes, no text, no decoration.
Inspired by Muji, Naoto Fukasawa and Jasper Morrison ("super normal").

**Use for:** organisers, holders, boxes, kitchen and bathroom things, objects without
electronics.
**Not for:** devices with controls or screens (→ `quiet`), gadgets (→ `instrument`).

## Principles

1. Super normal: it should feel obvious, as if it always existed.
2. Without thought: the hand finds the right way without labels.
3. No logo, no text, no decoration.
4. Soft, generous volumes; quiet proportions on a 10 mm grid.

## Form

- Large vertical corner radius (R8), soft top edges (fillet 1.6), bed chamfer 0.5.
- Thicker walls (2.4) and floor (2.0) for a solid feel.
- Flush faces; openings are simple and few.

## Interface

- If air or drainage is needed: sparse round holes Ø1.6 on a 4 mm grid.
- Grips and openings are shaped, not labelled.

## Graphics

- None.

## Colour, material, finish

- Bambu PLA Matte Black, one colour. No accent.

## Do / don't

- Do: simple volumes, soft edges, quiet proportions.
- Don't: text, logos, sharp details, visible fasteners.
