# quiet

Calm, durable objects: few elements, soft but precise edges, nothing extra.
Inspired by Dieter Rams and Braun ("Weniger, aber besser" — less, but better).

**Use for:** devices that live in the home — wall and desk displays, chargers, enclosures for
electronics, anything that should look calm for years.
**Not for:** playful handheld gadgets (→ `instrument`), simple holders (→ `plain`).

## Principles (from Rams' ten principles for good design)

1. Useful and understandable: the form explains how to use it.
2. Unobtrusive: a tool, not a sculpture.
3. Honest: no fake materials, no detail that pretends.
4. As little design as possible: every element must earn its place.
5. Long-lasting: no trends.

## Form

- Rectangular volumes with soft vertical corners (R3), a fillet on top edges (1.0), bed
  chamfer 0.5.
- Flush faces (no insets); one main face carries all controls and the display.
- Proportions on a 5 mm grid.

## Interface

- Grilles as parallel slots: 1.6 mm wide, 3.2 mm pitch, 20 mm long, on the grid.
- Controls are few, aligned on one line or a simple grid; displays sit in a clean rectangular
  window.

## Graphics

- IBM Plex Sans SemiBold, lowercase, engraved 0.6 mm, size ≥ 8 mm.
- Text only where it is needed to use the object.

## Colour, material, finish

- Bambu PLA Matte Black body, one colour per print.
- Optional accent: PLA Matte Red, separate part, **one** functional element only (like the
  coloured power switch on Braun devices).

## Do / don't

- Do: alignment, generous flat areas, one clear front.
- Don't: ornaments, visible branding, multiple accents, mixed edge styles.
